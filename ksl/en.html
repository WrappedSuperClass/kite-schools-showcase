04 / PRACTICAL INFORMATION

The details that matter.

Hours and season

School: daily by reservation; annual closure 15 December–15 January. Safety/rental: tramontane days, April–October, 09:00–17:00; weekends until 19:00; July–August until 20:00.

Official source ↗

Before you arrive

Confirm level, equipment, insurance and conditions with the school for lessons, or the club for independent riding. Ask the club about current options for adaptive sessions or handikite.

Official source ↗

The team

Youth team: April–October, Wednesdays and Saturdays; Saturdays only in July–August. Performance team: September–June, Wednesdays and Saturdays. Contact the club to join.
