05 / GOOD TO KNOW

Before you head out.

What's the difference between the club and the school?+

The school gives the lessons (kitesurf and wing, all levels, by reservation). The club runs the spot: safety, kite and SUP rental, teams, competition and club life. Two contacts, one home.

When is the safety / rental service open?+

Every tramontane wind day: April to October from 9 am to 5 pm, weekends until 7 pm, July and August until 8 pm.

How does handikite work?+

The handikite tandem opens up riding to people with disabilities, supervised by the club team. Contact the club to arrange an adapted session.

My child wants to progress towards competition, is that possible?+

Yes — it's even one of the club's specialties: beginner and performance youth teams, with a pathway to the kiteboard Pôle Espoir (national youth squad).
