The spot

The La Palme lagoon, the Aude's blue lagoon.

Between Leucate and Port-la-Nouvelle, the La Palme lagoon offers shallow water across hectares of flat water and wind conditions for every level. It's the perfect place to learn: you can stand, there's nothing to fear, and you progress fast.

  • Shallow water, flat water surface
  • Steady Tramontane wind from March to November
  • 10 min from the shop, 1 h 45 from Toulouse
  • Protected scenery of the Narbonnaise Natural Park

Frequently asked questions

Before you rig up.

What should I bring to the lesson?

Just your neoprene booties and sunglasses — everything else is provided: kite, board, helmet, harness, vest and wetsuit.

Do I need a licence or insurance?

The AFKite licence is required: €18, issued right on site on your first day. A medical certificate is required for riders over 59.

Can I ride in winter?

The school runs from March to November. Outside that window, contact the shop: some instructors stay available for sessions on demand.

Why learn on the lagoon rather than at sea?

Shallow water, flat surface, steady wind: the La Palme lagoon combines the three conditions that speed up learning — and put beginners at ease.
