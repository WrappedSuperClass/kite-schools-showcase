The spot

Rascasse Island, a 100% private spot.

Boat ride out to the island

Far from the crowded beaches, your lesson starts with a mini-cruise. On the island, you can stand up everywhere and there are no other riders: the perfect conditions to learn fast and with confidence, in one of the windiest and sunniest regions of France.

  • Boat access, coaching right by your side
  • Shallow water across the whole learning zone
  • Radio helmets: live instructions straight in your ear
  • About 1 h 50 from Toulouse, between Narbonne and Perpignan

Your first session

Three hours, one island, zero spectators.

Course set for the island

Gear up, hop aboard, briefing on the spot and the safety signals. Then static kite flying and discovering the wind window.

The waterstart

Directional body-dragging, relaunching the kite, then waterstart training — with standing depth at every break, you learn without fear.

Your first rides

First runs and riding upwind — all the way to jumps, rotations and strapless for the more advanced.

Frequently asked questions

Before you board.

Where is the meeting point?

At 22 avenue Cap de Front, 66420 Le Barcarès — on the border of Port Leucate. The boat leaves from there and reaches Rascasse Island in a few minutes.

Do I need to know how to swim?

Yes, but don't worry: all around the island you can stand up everywhere. The vest and radio helmet provided add a double layer of safety.

What does the price include?

All the gear: kite, board, wetsuit, harness, vest and helmet with radio — plus the boat ride to the spot. Just bring your booties if you have some.

Is the spot really private?

The island sits in the middle of the lagoon, reachable only by boat: during your lesson, there are no other riders in the zone. It's one of a kind in the region.

What about a group or a family?

The tribe / family package (3 h, 400 €) is made for exactly that, and the discovery for two (65 €/pers) lets you taste kitesurfing at a small price.
