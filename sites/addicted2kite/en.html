Frequently asked questions

We tell you everything.

How long does it take to learn kitesurfing?

Everyone progresses at their own pace: allow at least 3 sessions for the basics, 5 for solid foundations — kite control, wind, riding, safety, weather, gear. Independence comes after that.

Is riding in the sea difficult at Narbonne?

No: you start from the beach, work in the shallow-water zone, then use the open sea for your first rides. The boat picks you up and brings you back upwind.

From what age can kids take part?

It's weight that matters: 40 kg minimum. Small, low-pull kites and coaching adapted case by case. A swimming certificate and parental consent are required.

Do I need insurance?

Yes, kite/wing third-party liability insurance is mandatory at the school — your personal policies don't cover this sport. Online sign-up available, proof required at the start of your course.

Kitesurf or wingfoil — which should I choose?

Kitesurfing delivers thrills faster; the wing lets you ride in very little wind and on inland waters. At A2K, you can try both.
