The spot

From the lagoon to the sea, by boat.

The Sigean lagoon, shallow and sheltered, is the perfect cocoon for your first steps. Once you've mastered riding upwind, it's off to la Vieille Nouvelle beach, a wild gem between Gruissan and Port-la-Nouvelle, reachable only by boat.

  • Shallow water: you learn with your feet on the bottom
  • The unique scenery of the Narbonnaise Nature Park
  • Sea trips to a wild beach, far from the concrete
  • Water-sports base: parking, easy launch

Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

Why learn on a lagoon rather than at sea?

Shallow water, a sheltered spot, steady wind: you keep your feet on the bottom, you string exercises together without fear, and you progress much faster. The sea comes next — by boat.

What does a 4-hour session include?

Equipment prep, the boat ride out to the spot and time on the water. Full equipment provided — bring a swimsuit, a towel and sunscreen.

What if there's no wind?

The session is rescheduled to suit your availability. On site, kayak tours through the nature park make a lovely plan B to explore the lagoon's ecosystem.

How can I pay?

Card, cash, cheques, French holiday vouchers and bank transfers are accepted. Third-party kite liability insurance is required and not included.
