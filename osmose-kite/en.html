The spots

4 spots, every wind, zero wasted sessions.

Based at Coussoules Basses in La Franqui, on the huge Mondial du Vent beach. Depending on the wind's direction and strength, Osmose switches between 4 different spots to give you the day's best conditions — sea, lagoon, flat water or small waves.

  • 8 km of wild beach at Les Coussoules
  • Shallow water to start with confidence
  • Tramontane and sea breeze: it blows almost all year round
  • ~1 h 45 from Toulouse, WeRide partner shop in the village

Frequently asked questions

Before you take off.

How does end-of-course payment work?

You book your sessions, you ride… and you only settle up at the end, only for the time actually spent practising with decent wind. No wind = no bill, and nothing to catch up.

What happens on windless days?

Either we don't practise (and you pay nothing), or we switch to mountainboard, powerkite or the simulator to keep progressing on dry land.

Is the video analysis really included?

Yes — your sessions are filmed, reviewed with you, and you keep the videos as a souvenir. It's included in every lesson.

Can you practise in winter?

Yes, Osmose is open all year round, with a winter rate of 100 € for the 4 h session (1 December – 15 March). Warm wetsuit provided on request.

What about groups?

From 3 people: corporate groups, stag/hen parties, schools, camps — tailor-made quotes with preferential rates.
