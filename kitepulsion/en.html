04 / YOUR PLAYGROUND

Les CoussoulesLa Franqui

In La Franqui, between the cliff and the dunes, Les Coussoules beach stretches out over 8 kilometres of unspoiled sand . This is where the Mondial du Vent is held: huge open space, shallow water along the shore, steady tramontane from April to October.

  • Favourable wind statistics from April to October
  • Shallow-water learning zone
  • Open water with Zodiac support
  • ~1 h 45 from Toulouse, 30 min from Perpignan

05 / GOOD TO KNOW

Before you head out.

What if there's no wind during my course?+

The session is postponed at the instructor's discretion, or refunded. Postponed sessions stay valid for one year — if you can, plan a spare day into your stay.

What time does the lesson take place?+

The time and meeting point are confirmed the evening before at 8 pm, after the last weather bulletin, to hit the best wind window.

What should I bring?+

A full neoprene wetsuit (loan possible, mention your size when booking), neoprene booties, sunglasses, sunscreen, a snack and water.

Is insurance included?+

No — the FFVL kite-sports licence is required: from 7.50 € for 2 or 9 days, 25 € for the year. Take it out online before the course to save time on day one.

I'm already independent — what do you offer?+

Performance coaching in groups of 4 to 6: freestyle, strapless, foil, video shooting and analysis. And in winter, snowkiting takes over.
