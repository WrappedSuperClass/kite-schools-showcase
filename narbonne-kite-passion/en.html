01 / THE SPIRIT OF THE SCHOOL

Something good starts here.
Another kind of freedom

NKP has welcomed students at La Nautique since 2011. Lessons take place in shallow water, with each student using their own kite and radio channel. Changing rooms, toilets and hot showers are listed on site.

04 / PRACTICAL INFORMATION

The details that matter.

Hours and season

Lessons: 09:00–13:00 or 14:00–18:00; published meeting times 08:45 or 13:45. Contact hours: daily 08:30–19:30. Pages disagree on season end; check your dates in the schedule.

Official source ↗

Before you arrive

Specific insurance is compulsory and excluded. Courses include personal equipment, kite equipment and boat transport. A 4-hour session includes setup and packing; published water time is 2–2½ hours.

Official source ↗

Prices and conditions

Paired prices show low season, then July–August. The site also lists stand-up paddle through La Nautique base. Check each activity’s own conditions.
