Three minutes from exit 38 on the A9 and ten minutes from Gruissan, the la Nautique base opens onto a shallow, flat and immense stretch of water used exclusively by the school. The boat drops you on the sandbank: all that's left to do is fly.

05 / GOOD TO KNOW

Before you head out.

What if there's no wind on the day of my lesson?+

The lesson is cancelled and rescheduled around your availability — that's the NKP wind guarantee. You never pay for a session you can't ride.

Do I need to know how to swim? Is there a minimum weight?+

Being able to swim is essential. Kitesurfing is possible from around 45 kg. No other particular physical condition is required to get started.

How does booking work?+

Check availability, sign up online or by phone, and receive your confirmation by email. The school is open 7 days a week from March to November, 8:30 am – 7:30 pm.

Is the gear provided?+

Yes, everything is included: current-year Duotone kite and board, wetsuit, harness, impact vest and radio helmet. Just bring a swimsuit, a towel and sunscreen.

Can two people with different levels come together?+

Yes — each student has their own kite and their own radio channel, so the instructor adapts the coaching to each of you in real time.
