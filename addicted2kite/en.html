01 / THE SPIRIT OF THE SCHOOL

Something good starts here.
Another kind of freedom

Founded in 2015 by Julien and Laurent, Addicted2kite offers beginner lessons, progression and supervised rental. The official site lists at most four students per instructor, nearby parking, rinsing facilities, a shop and a relaxation area.

03 / YOUR PLAYGROUND

Le Créneau NaturelNarbonne-Plage

The base is at Le Créneau Naturel, D332, 11100 Narbonne-Plage, opposite Aquajet. The second spot is La Vieille Nouvelle near Gruissan. Boats provide recovery and upwind transport.

04 / PRACTICAL INFORMATION

The details that matter.

Before you arrive

Published minimum weight: 40 kg, subject to individual assessment. Children need swimming attestation and parental authorisation. Kite/wing liability insurance is compulsory; bring proof for the start of the course.
