05 / GOOD TO KNOW

Before you head out.

Where is the meeting point?+

At 22 avenue Cap de Front, 66420 Le Barcarès — on the border of Port Leucate. The boat leaves from there and reaches Rascasse Island in a few minutes.

Do I need to know how to swim?+

Yes, but don't worry: all around the island you can stand up everywhere. The vest and radio helmet provided add a double layer of safety.

What does the price include?+

All the gear: kite, board, wetsuit, harness, vest and helmet with radio — plus the boat ride to the spot. Just bring your booties if you have some.

Is the spot really private?+

The island sits in the middle of the lagoon, reachable only by boat: during your lesson, there are no other riders in the zone. It's one of a kind in the region.

What about a group or a family?+

The tribe / family package (3 h, 400 €) is made for exactly that, and the discovery for two (65 €/pers) lets you taste kitesurfing at a small price.
