04 / PRACTICAL INFORMATION

The details that matter.

Prices and conditions

€130 per person outside July–August; €150 in July–August for the à-la-carte and flexible options.

Official source ↗

Before you arrive

Specific FFVL licence/insurance is compulsory and excluded. Check current cover and fees before the course. Bring neoprene boots, sun protection, water and a snack. Request a wetsuit loan when registering.

Official source ↗

Hours and season

Unsuitable weather: postponement or refund. Weather-related unused sessions remain valid for one year. Powerkite, catakite and boat-towed activities are also offered, with snowkite in winter.
