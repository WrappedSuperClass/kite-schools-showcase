04 / PRACTICAL INFORMATION

The details that matter.

Hours and season

High season: July and August. Low season: 15 March–30 June and 1 September–15 November. Winter: 1 December–15 March, according to the official sheet. Confirm lesson slots by phone.

Official source ↗

Before you arrive

Specific liability cover is compulsory. The booking page links licence documents and parental authorisation for minors. Check current insurance fees before enrolling.
