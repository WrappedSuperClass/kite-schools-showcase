01 / THE SPIRIT OF THE SCHOOL

Something good starts here.
Another kind of freedom

Luc Graulou adapts progression to your board-sports experience. The simulator lets you practise foil balance without handling a kite or wing. The school then offers wingfoil and kitefoil sessions and packages.
