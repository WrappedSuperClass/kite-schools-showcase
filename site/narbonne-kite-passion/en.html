02 / THE SCHOOL

Something good starts here.
Another kind of freedom

Four students at most, each with a kite and radio. Reach Étang de Bages by boat, then warm up with a hot shower.

Meeting point+

La Nautique, rue des Nautiquards. Meet at 08:45 or 13:45. Confirm the exact meeting point with the team.

Get directions ↗
What to bring+

Equipment and radio included. Compulsory kite insurance costs extra. Bring water, sunscreen and swimwear.

Prices and hours+

Sessions run 09:00–13:00 or 14:00–18:00 including setup: around 2–2.5 hours on the water. High season is July–August.
