04 / PRACTICAL INFORMATION

The details that matter.

Before you arrive

For kitesurf, the base states that supervised riding is compulsory on this spot. Confirm your level, included equipment, insurance, duration and option conditions in the portal or by phone.

Official source ↗

Equipment and facilities

The official page lists Duotone and Fanatic equipment renewed each season. Pôle Mer and Pôle Étang are separate bases: Mateille caters in particular to families and beginner wingfoil.

Official source ↗

Prices and conditions

Prices, dates and durations depend on the activity selected in the official portal. Pôle Mer also lists corporate and team activities for groups up to 40 people, on enquiry.

05 / GOOD TO KNOW

Before you head out.

How do I book?+

On the official booking page, choose “Réserver au Pôle Mer” for Gruissan. It opens that base’s Activiteez portal. For Mateille, choose Pôle Étang. If the portal does not load, call the base.
Official source ↗
