04 / PRACTICAL INFORMATION

The details that matter.

Prices and conditions

High season: July–August; the first amount is low season. Group sessions last 4 hours including setup and boat transport. Lesson prices include equipment.

Official source ↗

Before you arrive

Specific kitesurf insurance is compulsory. Complete the official registration form, including after telephone booking, and confirm participation requirements with the school.

Hours and season

Lessons take place and are charged when conditions allow. The official FAQ provides postponement or refund if you leave after weather cancellation. Listed payments: card, cash, cheques, holiday vouchers and instant bank transfer.
