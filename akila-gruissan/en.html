05 / GOOD TO KNOW

Before you head out.

What times are the kitesurfing lessons?+

Morning sessions (from 9 am) or afternoon depending on the weather, plus 2-hour evening coaching for independent riders with a specific goal.

I'm an independent rider — can I ride freely?+

On this spot, boat-supervised riding is mandatory for kitesurfing. Rentals include this year's Duotone gear; a level check and a deposit may be required.

Can my kids get started?+

From age 5 at the Jardin des Mers (étang de Mateille): a playful discovery of the water, then optimist, catamaran, windsurfing and wingfoil as they grow.

What about no-wind days?+

Paddle, kayak and catamaran at the Pôle Étang — and the seafront restaurant to relive past sessions and plan the next ones.
