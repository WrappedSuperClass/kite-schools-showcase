## Production boundary
No provider account, checkout, database, availability feed, form delivery, tracking or email-send integration was created. Telephone/email/map links are real; the form uses no network or persistent storage. The root remains blank. Existing ten school sites and their localized pages remain untouched.

Generated visuals are atmospheric illustrations, not documentary photographs of a school, instructor, exact lesson location or its equipment. Each page identifies this in the image alternative text and its short footer note. No invented reviews, certifications, weather, real-time availability or operator portraits.

For later live scheduling, the first recommendation is to retain each school's existing booking provider. AirXKite currently embeds Regiondo; its current pricing is quote-based (https://go.regiondo.com/pricing). For a school needing a simpler new embedded scheduler, Cal.com is a suitable off-the-shelf option: Individual free, Teams $12 per user/month billed annually (https://cal.com/pricing, checked 11 September 2026). Neither service was activated or purchased; the requested prototypes stay self-contained.
