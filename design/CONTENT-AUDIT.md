Current factual issues and limits:

- **SkyFly’s meeting address needs correction before relying on the page for arrival instructions.**
- **Coriolis’s monetary price section was lost**, even though the three descriptive course entries survived.
- **KSL’s text refers to two contacts while showing only the school contact.**
- Narbonne Kite Passion’s own pages conflict on season end (March–December versus March–November) and distinguish a 12 rue des Nautiquards meeting address from a 38 rue des Nautiquards footer address. Clarify with the school before asserting one precise arrival location or season. Its customer-contact hours are also not the same as lesson-slot hours.
- Addicted2kite’s linked tariff page is dated 2022. Chinook’s school-specific evidence located in this audit is dated 2024. Neither establishes all current prices or insurance conditions.
- Several inherited promotional/safety statements, travel-time estimates, annual wind-day figures and insurance terms were not independently substantiated. Copying a FAQ is not factual verification.
- No enquiry, registration, booking, payment or message to a school was submitted. Calendar presence is separated from working availability and completed checkout. NKP and Akila dynamic availability could not be verified in this session.

Recommended order of correction: fix arrival/contact contradictions; restore booking/registration deep links to the schools’ existing systems; restore omitted prices, insurance/deposit instructions and practical spot information with dated sources; then reconcile every language and retest the booking handoffs.
